About Kite in Morocco

Our School History

Since 2008, Kite in Morocco has been the premier kitesurf school in the region. We provide top-quality instruction and training for all skill levels. Our mission is to share the passion and excitement of kitesurf with everyone who visits our beautiful coastline.

Kite in Morocco is dedicated to providing the safest and most enjoyable kitesurf experience. With professional instructors and world-class equipment, we ensure every student reaches their full potential.

Why Choose Kite in Morocco
