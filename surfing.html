Why Surf in Essaouira?

Essaouira is one of Morocco's most consistent and beginner-friendly surf destinations. With year-round swell, uncrowded breaks, and beautiful beaches, it's the perfect place to learn and improve your surfing skills.

  • Consistent waves - Year-round Atlantic swells
  • Beginner-friendly - Multiple beach breaks suitable for all levels
  • Less crowded - Plenty of space for learning
  • Beautiful setting - Stunning coastal scenery and culture
