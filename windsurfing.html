What is Windsurfing?

Windsurfing is a combination of surfing and sailing, offering an incredible balance between technical skill and physical challenge. It's perfect for those seeking an exciting, dynamic water sport that engages both mind and body.

  • Versatile sport - From beginners to professional racers
  • Full-body workout - Builds strength and endurance
  • Challenging & fun - Progress quickly with good instruction
  • Beautiful experience - Sail Essaouira's stunning coastline

Our Windsurfing Program

1
Beach Theory

Learn equipment components, wind interpretation, and safety procedures on the beach.

2
Water Entry

Begin in shallow water, practicing balance and getting up on the board.

3
Sailing Skills

Progress to sailing techniques, maneuvering, and controlling speed and direction.

4
Advanced Techniques

Master jibing, tacking, wave riding, and freestyle tricks.
